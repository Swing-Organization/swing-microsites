﻿Swing × John Montgomery
The Sunday Edit · Creative brief
Campaign idea
Swing’s modern, fashion-forward approach to women’s golf meets John Montgomery’s iconic, classic preppy style. The Sunday Edit brings together fresh proportions, confident styling, and the familiar details of golf’s wardrobe: crisp collars, stripes, plaid, knitwear, and tailored silhouettes.
The story is about a woman making the game her own. A Sunday on the course becomes a chance to express her style, spend time with her people, and enjoy the ritual of playing. The creative should make that world feel aspirational, personal, and inviting.
Campaign line: “Forget the flowers. Book the tee time.”
Internal reference cues from marketing: Malbon for Swing’s contemporary golf-fashion sensibility; Ralph Lauren for John Montgomery’s classic preppy sensibility. Develop an original identity for the collaboration.
Creative objectives
* Make both collaborators recognizable in one coherent creative world: Swing’s artistic, modern energy and John Montgomery’s polished preppy signature.
* Create the feeling of entering a fashion editorial, with campaign photography and film leading the experience.
* Help women picture themselves wearing the collection on the course and throughout the rest of their day.
Creative success means a visitor understands the collaboration at a glance, feels drawn into its world, and wants to explore the pieces.
Audience insights
Our audience is women who enjoy golf and care about fashion, including newer players developing their own relationship with the sport.
* Desires: a distinctive personal style, beautifully considered clothing, and a sense of belonging on the course.
* Tensions: golf can feel intimidating; traditional styling can feel corny and old school; functional clothing can leave little room for self-expression.
* Motivations: discovering a designer collaboration, putting together a look that feels individual and high end, and making time for the game and the people she enjoys.
Emotional takeaway: “This feels fashion forward. I can actually love my look while I am on the course.”
Tone and voice
Confident, chic, warm, and lightly witty. The campaign has the polish of a fashion label and the ease of a good Sunday with friends. Let the headline carry the playful attitude; keep supporting copy spare and assured.
* Use short, conversational phrases with a strong point of view.
* Make classic preppy style feel welcoming and current. Avoid exclusive-club language, heavy golf jargon, and overly nostalgic copy.
* Keep the emphasis on fashion, classic looks modernized, and the pleasure of playing.
Headline direction: “Forget the flowers. Book the tee time.”
Supporting copy direction: “Introducing The Sunday Edit. Swing’s modern point of view meets John Montgomery’s classic style.”
Calls to action: “Explore the collection” and “Shop the edit.”
Visual guidelines
Color
Navy #14263D and green #24533E anchor the campaign. White #FFFFFF creates crisp contrast and generous breathing room. Brown #76543C adds warmth in small accents and photographic details, such as leather accessories or clubhouse wood.
Keep navy, green, and white dominant. Brown should be a supporting note.
Typography and graphic language
Pair a refined sign paper house script for campaign headlines with a clean Baskerville-style serif for supporting copy and navigation. Use large, expressive type, disciplined spacing, thin rules, and occasional stripe details. Keep collaboration branding simple and prominent.
Photography, styling, and film
All photography and video must come exclusively from the campaign assets supplied by Swing’s marketing team in Google Drive. Do not generate new images or videos or source substitute visuals elsewhere. Select from the supplied assets to balance considered fashion compositions, candid moments of movement and connection, full looks, and close-ups of texture, layering, and classic preppy details.
Present the supplied imagery in a way that feels high end, artistic, and contemporary. Use layout, scale, and spacing to highlight the existing styling and connect the photographs to the navy, green, white, and brown page palette.
Use only the supplied campaign film and video clips to establish atmosphere. Give imagery room to breathe; transitions should feel quiet and deliberate. If an image or video needed for the layout is missing from Google Drive, flag it for the marketing team to supply. Use all images provided some where on the page. This should feel like a collection page, magazine style display of the campaign. 
Microsite layout
Create one continuous page with three main sections. The journey should move from feeling, to story, to product discovery.
1. Campaign opening — the visual world
Open with a large campaign film or hero image from the marketing team’s Google Drive assets. Feature Swing × John Montgomery, The Sunday Edit, and the campaign headline. An “Explore the collection” call to action invites visitors further into the page.
Follow the opening with an editorial arrangement of campaign photographs and video moments. Mix full-width imagery with offset crops and white space. Let the styling and atmosphere lead.
2. About the campaign — the collaboration story
Pair a short story about the meeting of the two brands with a complementary campaign image. Explain how Swing’s modern fashion perspective brings new expression to John Montgomery’s classic preppy vocabulary. Keep this section intimate and concise.
3. Products — shop the edit
Present a curated selection from the collaboration in a clean, spacious product section. Balance clear product imagery with a small amount of copy and a direct shopping call to action. Each featured piece should lead to its product page on Swing’s main website.
The product selection and supporting product information will be supplied separately. Images are to be found in the Product Images folder inside the campaign folder. Product details are on the Products: Swing x John Montgomery sheet inside the campaign folder. 
Deliverables and supplied creative
Deliver one campaign microsite with desktop and mobile layouts covering the campaign opening, collaboration story, and product section. The creative treatment should include page design, concise campaign copy, and placement of supplied photography and video.
Marketing will supply campaign photography and film through Google Drive. Use these assets to establish a consistent visual story across the page. Google Drive campaign asset folder: to be supplied.
